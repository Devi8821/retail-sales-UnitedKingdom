# 🛍️ UK Retail Sales Analytics (2019)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes retail e-commerce transaction data from a UK-based retailer to uncover sales trends, customer purchasing behavior, product performance, and retention patterns. The objective is to transform raw sales data into actionable business insights that support strategic decision-making in marketing, pricing, inventory management, and customer retention.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze monthly and seasonal sales trends
- Identify top-performing products and revenue drivers
- Understand customer purchasing behavior
- Measure customer retention using Cohort Analysis
- Evaluate price sensitivity through Price Elasticity
- Provide data-driven business recommendations

# 📊 Key Findings

### 📈 Seasonal Sales Performance

Sales remained relatively stable throughout most of the year before reaching a significant peak in **November 2019**, generating approximately **£7.86 million** in revenue.

**Business Insight**

Holiday campaigns and seasonal demand have a major impact on revenue growth.

---

### 🛒 Product Performance

- Household and seasonal products dominate sales.
- Only a small portion of products contributes to the majority of revenue (Pareto Principle).

**Business Insight**

Focus inventory and marketing investment on high-performing products while optimizing the remaining product portfolio.

---

### 💰 Price Elasticity

Customers are highly price-sensitive.

The optimal selling price is approximately **£6–£7**, where purchase volume reaches its highest level.

**Business Insight**

Use promotional pricing and product bundling to maintain demand for higher-priced products.

---

### 👥 Customer Retention

The **January 2019 cohort** achieved the highest Month-1 retention rate at **33.5%**, while most customers completed only **1–5 transactions**.

**Business Insight**

Customer acquisition is strong, but repeat purchasing remains an opportunity for improvement.

---

### 🌍 Geographic Performance

The United Kingdom contributes the majority of total revenue, while the Netherlands, Australia, and Singapore represent promising international markets.

**Business Insight**

Expand marketing efforts in high-value international markets while maintaining strong domestic performance.

---

# 💡 Business Recommendations

- Strengthen seasonal campaigns during November–December.
- Introduce loyalty programs and personalized offers to improve customer retention.
- Implement product bundling and promotional pricing strategies.
- Prioritize inventory planning before peak shopping seasons.
- Expand marketing initiatives in high-potential international markets.
- Increase weekend promotional campaigns to capitalize on higher customer activity.
